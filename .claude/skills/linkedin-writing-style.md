# LinkedIn Writing Style

A LinkedIn writing style that balances authority with accessibility. Optimized for engagement while maintaining professional credibility.

## Core Principles

### 1. Authority Without Arrogance
- Write from experience, not theory
- Share specific examples and data points
- Acknowledge what you don't know
- Credit others generously

### 2. Accessibility Over Jargon
- Use simple, direct language
- Explain technical concepts in plain terms
- Write like you're talking to a smart friend over coffee
- No unnecessary buzzwords or corporate speak

### 3. Contrarian but Not Combative
- Challenge conventional wisdom when it's wrong
- Back up contrarian takes with reasoning
- Respectfully disagree with popular opinions
- Focus on ideas, not attacking people

### 4. Story-Driven, Not Preachy
- Start with a specific moment or realization
- Use concrete examples over abstract advice
- Show, don't tell
- Let readers draw their own conclusions

## Post Structure Patterns

### Pattern 1: The Specific Realization
```
[Specific moment or observation]

This made me realize [insight].

Here's why it matters:
• [Point 1]
• [Point 2]
• [Point 3]

[Actionable takeaway]
```

### Pattern 2: The Data-Driven Insight
```
[Surprising statistic or finding]

Everyone says [conventional wisdom].
But the data shows [contrarian insight].

Here's what actually works:
1. [Tactical point]
2. [Tactical point]
3. [Tactical point]

[Call to action or question]
```

### Pattern 3: The Behind-the-Scenes
```
Most people think [common belief].

Here's what actually happens behind the scenes:
[Insider perspective with specifics]

The takeaway:
[Actionable insight]
```

### Pattern 4: The List Format
```
[Bold claim or pattern recognition]

[Number] things I've learned:

1. [Lesson with specific example]
2. [Lesson with specific example]
3. [Lesson with specific example]
...

[Synthesis or question to audience]
```

## Opening Hooks That Work

### The Unexpected Stat
- "95% of [common activity] fails. Here's the 5% that works:"
- "I analyzed [number] [things]. Only [small number] had [result]."
- "After [large number] of [activity], I've noticed [pattern]."

### The Contrarian Take
- "Everyone says [popular advice]. I think it's wrong."
- "The opposite of [conventional wisdom] is actually true."
- "Here's why [popular strategy] is overrated:"

### The Specific Moment
- "Yesterday at [time], [specific thing happened]."
- "I just reviewed [specific number] of [things]. One pattern stood out:"
- "Three years ago I [decision]. Here's what happened:"

### The Pattern Recognition
- "I keep seeing the same mistake in [field]:"
- "After working with [number] [people/companies], I've noticed:"
- "There's a pattern in [industry] that nobody talks about:"

## Content Themes That Resonate

### Behind-the-Scenes Knowledge
- How things actually work vs. how people think they work
- Insider perspectives from your field
- The unsexy parts that matter more than the sexy parts

### Anti-Advice (What NOT to Do)
- Common mistakes and why they happen
- Overrated strategies
- Conventional wisdom that's wrong

### Specific Tactical Insights
- Exact processes, frameworks, templates
- Numbers, timelines, specific tools
- Repeatable methods with clear steps

### Personal Lessons (But Make Them Universal)
- Failures and what you learned
- Unexpected turns that taught you something
- Moments that changed your perspective

## Writing Mechanics

### Sentence Structure
- Short sentences create punch
- Vary length for rhythm
- One idea per sentence
- Break paragraphs frequently (2-3 lines max on mobile)

### Word Choice
- Active voice beats passive
- Specific beats vague ("3 weeks" beats "quickly")
- Concrete beats abstract ("PostgreSQL query" beats "database operation")
- Remove unnecessary words ruthlessly

### Formatting for Skimmers
- Use bullets and numbers liberally
- Bold key phrases (sparingly)
- White space is your friend
- Front-load the value (don't bury the lead)

## Engagement Tactics

### Questions That Drive Comments
- Ask for specific examples: "What's the worst [X] you've seen?"
- Request contrarian opinions: "Am I wrong about this?"
- Invite pattern sharing: "Have you noticed this too?"
- Seek tactical input: "What would you add?"

### Controversy Without Trolling
- Disagree with ideas, not people
- Provide reasoning, not just hot takes
- Acknowledge valid counterarguments
- Be willing to update your view

### Generosity and Credit
- Tag people you learned from
- Share others' great work
- Give credit for ideas that influenced you
- Build others up authentically

## What to Avoid

### Cringe Factors
- ❌ Fake humility ("I'm just a simple [role]...")
- ❌ Humble-bragging
- ❌ Overused emoji combinations
- ❌ "Let that sink in" / "Read that again"
- ❌ Manufactured urgency
- ❌ Vague inspirational platitudes

### Engagement Bait
- ❌ "Agree?" posts with nothing substantive
- ❌ Recycled motivational quotes
- ❌ Polls just for algorithm gaming
- ❌ "Comment [word] for [thing]"

### Authenticity Killers
- ❌ Writing in a voice that's not yours
- ❌ Sharing advice you don't actually follow
- ❌ Posting just to post (quality over consistency)
- ❌ Pretending expertise you don't have

## Post Length Guidelines

### Short (100-200 words)
- Quick insights and observations
- Contrarian one-liners with brief explanation
- Links to external content with commentary

### Medium (200-400 words)
- Most posts should be this length
- Enough depth to be valuable, short enough to finish
- 3-5 tactical points or lessons

### Long (400-800 words)
- Deep dives and comprehensive guides
- Behind-the-scenes stories with lessons
- Only when the value justifies the length

### Very Long (800+ words)
- Rare, only for exceptional content
- Break into sections with clear headers
- Consider writing a LinkedIn article instead

## The Algorithm Plays

### What LinkedIn Rewards
- Native content (not links)
- Conversation (comments > likes)
- Dwell time (people reading to the end)
- Recency (post within 1-2 hours of peak audience time)

### Timing Strategy
- Weekday mornings (7-9am) and lunch (12-1pm) work well
- Avoid weekends for professional content
- Test your audience's patterns
- Consistency matters more than frequency

### Hashtag Strategy
- 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Mix of popular and niche
- Industry-specific tags
- Avoid generic mega-tags (#marketing)

## Authenticity Check

Before posting, ask:
1. Would I say this in a real conversation?
2. Am I sharing actual experience or repeating generic advice?
3. Is this adding new perspective or just noise?
4. Would I respect someone else posting this?

If any answer is "no," revise or don't post.

## Remember

The best LinkedIn content sounds like you, not like "LinkedIn content." Write with authority earned through experience, share generously, and focus on being useful rather than impressive.
