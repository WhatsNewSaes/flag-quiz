Flag Arcade turns world flags and geography into a set of fast, retro-inspired games.

Play through Journey Mode, sharpen your skills in Arcade Mode, race Around the World, test yourself in Flag Jeopardy, dodge wrong answers in Flag Runner, or try to build the Perfect Passport in a 10-question world tour challenge.

Features:
- Learn flags from 197 countries
- Multiple game modes for quick sessions or longer practice

## Google Play

- Full description:

Flag Arcade is a retro-style flag quiz game with multiple ways to learn, practice, and challenge yourself.

Build your skills across 197 country flags with game modes made for quick mobile play:
- Journey Mode: progress through worlds, earn stars, and unlock achievements
- Arcade Mode: customize your quiz and practice at your pace
- Around the World: race through flags from every continent
- Flag Jeopardy: choose easy multiple choice or type the answer
- Flag Runner: collect flags in a pixel-art platformer
- Perfect Passport: answer 10 geography questions and share your score

Whether you are studying geography, practicing country flags, or just want a fun trivia challenge, Flag Arcade gives you a colorful world tour in your pocket.
